About

Three crisp cans, presented in 3D.

CRISP is a small web app that puts three flavoured crisp cans into a browser-based 3D viewer. You can rotate them, change the lighting, switch the camera angle, and inspect the wireframe.

The app

The viewer is built with Three.js. The three cans are loaded as .glb files exported from Blender. A GLTF loader pulls each model into the scene, an environment map adds soft reflections, and the user controls drive lights and camera presets.

Orbit controls handle drag, zoom and pan. GSAP animates the camera between preset angles. The Spin button rotates the active model a full turn so you can quickly see the artwork from every side.

The models

BBQ
Smoky barbecue. Deep magenta can with charred edges and a hint of sweet spice.
French Onion
Creamy french onion dip. Fresh green can with a savoury, herby finish.
Paprika
Bright paprika. Warm orange can with a peppery, smoked finish.

Controls

  • Drag to rotate. Scroll to zoom. Right-click drag to pan.
  • Flavour buttons switch the active can.
  • Wireframe toggles the mesh view. Auto-rotate keeps the can turning. Spin plays a one-shot full turn.
  • Ambient, Key and Rim light each toggle a separate light group.
  • Camera presets jump to Front, Top, Side or Reset, eased with GSAP.

Statement of originality

The brand CRISP, the page layout, the copy, the CSS, the JavaScript and the 3D scene setup were produced by the author for this assignment. The can base meshes were sourced from Sketchfab under their respective licences, then re-textured and re-tuned in Blender. No assets or code from the lab brand exercises were carried into this submission.
